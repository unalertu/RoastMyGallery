# Support — Roast My Gallery

Need help with **Roast My Gallery**? Email us and we'll get back to you.

**[roastmygallery@gmail.com](mailto:roastmygallery@gmail.com)**

We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days. It helps if
you include your **app version** — you'll find it in the app under
Settings → About → Version — along with your **iOS version** and a short
description of what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

### I bought gems but my balance didn't go up

Purchases are processed by Apple and the gems are added by our purchase
provider, so there can be a short delay. Try this in order:

1. Fully close the app and reopen it — the balance is refreshed on launch.
2. Tap your gem count (top right of Home), then **Restore** at the bottom of
   that screen.
3. Still missing? Email us with the date of the purchase and we'll sort it out.

### I got a new phone — where did my gems go?

Your gem balance is tied to an anonymous ID kept in your **iCloud Keychain**, so
it follows you to a new device automatically as long as iCloud Keychain is
turned on (iOS Settings → tap your name → iCloud, then check that Passwords /
Keychain syncing is enabled). If it was off when you set up the new phone, email
us and we'll help recover the balance.

### How do I get a refund?

All payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple — not by us.
Request one at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.
If a feature charged you but failed to deliver, email us: we'll restore the gems
even though we can't issue money back.

### The analysis says it can't find any photos

The app analyzes a slice of your library at a time — one month or album for a
Standard analysis, a date range for a Deep one — so an empty result usually
means that particular slice has no photos in it. Pick a different range, or grant
**Full Access** to your library (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos →
Roast My Gallery) — with *Limited* access, albums stay hidden from the app.

### Something the AI wrote was offensive or wrong

Tap **Report this analysis** at the bottom of any results screen. It opens a
pre-filled email containing the text in question so we can see exactly what
happened and tighten the writing rules that produced it. You can edit or delete
anything in that draft before sending it.

The stories are AI-generated for entertainment and can be wrong. They are never
advice of any kind.

### Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Not on a standard analysis — your photos are analyzed **on your device**, and
only anonymous statistics are sent to write your story. Photos leave your phone
only for the optional paid Deep Analysis captions, and only after you have seen
the exact photos and approved them — you can remove any of them or decline
entirely. They are never stored on our servers. The full detail is in the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/), and Settings → Privacy → **Review what data was
sent** shows the exact payload from your last analysis.

### How do I delete my data?

Everything the app saves lives on your device. Settings → Data → **Delete all
analysis history** removes it, and deleting the app removes the rest. We hold no
copy of your analyses.
